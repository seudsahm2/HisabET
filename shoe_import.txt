🚢 Shipping and Logistics
Most sea freight enters Ethiopia through Djibouti, followed by inland transport via the Ethio-Djibouti railway or truck to dry ports like Modjo.

Time: 25 to 45 days total (including inland transit).

📈 Step-by-Step Import Process
Business Setup: Obtain a TIN, VAT certificate, and a valid Import Trade License.
Proforma Invoice: Get this from your Chinese supplier to start the bank process.
Forex Application: Submit the proforma to your bank to request foreign currency; this can take time due to high demand.
Bank Permit: Once Forex is approved, the bank issues a permit to pay the supplier (usually via Letter of Credit or CAD).
Inspection: Arrange for a pre-shipment inspection in China to get the CIQ certificate.
Shipping: Supplier ships the goods and sends the Bill of Lading and other original documents to your bank.
Customs Clearance: Use a licensed clearing agent to file the Electronic Customs Declaration via the ESW Portal and pay all assessed duties.

When shoes arrive from China to Ethiopia, they are rarely packed as individual pairs in a loose pile. They follow a strict professional packing system designed for easy counting, customs inspection, and fast-paced "bundle selling" in markets like Merkato.
Here is the breakdown of how these cartons are organized and identified.
📦 The Master Carton (Outer Box)
Most imports use a "Standard Export Carton." This is a heavy-duty corrugated box designed to survive the sea journey.
Dimensions: Usually 60cm x 40cm x 50cm or similar.
Weight: Typically 15kg to 25kg depending on the shoe type (sneakers are lighter than boots).
Capacity: A standard carton usually contains 24, 30, 36, or 48 pairs.
🏷️ Shipping Marks (The "ID Card")
Importers and bundle sellers don't open the box to know what’s inside. They look at the Shipping Marks printed on the side:
Style Number/Model: (e.g., "Model 8802-Black")
Size Run: Usually written as "40-45" (for men) or "36-41" (for women).
Quantity: Written as "30 PRS" (Pairs).
Gross/Net Weight: (G.W./N.W.).
CBM: The volume (e.g., 0.12).
Brand Name: Often a local brand name registered by the Ethiopian importer.
👟 Internal Packaging & Differentiation
Within the master carton, items are differentiated to prevent damage and help sellers sort them quickly.
1. Individual Packaging
Boxed (High Quality): Each pair is in its own branded shoebox. These take up more space (fewer pairs per container) but sell for higher prices.
Polybagged (Economy): Most common for "bundle" sellers. Each pair is in a clear plastic bag with a silica gel packet. This maximizes space.
2. The "Size Curve" (Pre-Packing)
Suppliers in China pack by a "Size Curve" or "Assortment." A 30-pair carton is not 30 of the same size. It is usually:
Size 40: 3 pairs
Size 41: 6 pairs
Size 42: 9 pairs (The most popular size)
Size 43: 6 pairs
Size 44: 3 pairs
Size 45: 3 pairs
🇪🇹 How Ethiopian Bundle Sellers Buy
In Ethiopian markets like Merkato (Addis Ketema) or Megenagna, "bundle sellers" (wholesalers) buy these cartons in bulk. Here is what they look for to identify the items:
The "Color Code": Cartons are often marked with a colored sticker or a large letter (e.g., "B" for Black, "W" for White) so they can stack them in warehouses and find colors without opening boxes.
Sample Shoe: Importers usually take one pair out of one carton and tape it to the outside of the stack. This allows the bundle seller to see the physical quality, texture, and stitching before buying the whole "bundle" (stack of cartons).
Carton Serial Numbers: Sellers look for sequential numbers (e.g., Box 1 of 500) to ensure they are getting a complete shipment of a specific style.
The "Feel" of the Weight: Experienced sellers can often tell if a carton is "empty" or has missing pairs just by tilting or lifting it, as Chinese factory-packed cartons have a very specific, tight weight distribution.
