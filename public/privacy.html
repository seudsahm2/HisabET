Your ledger data stays purpose-driven and service-focused.

This policy explains what HisabET collects, how it is used, and how it supports authentication, reconciliation, and account security.

Section 01

Information We Collect

We may collect the following information to operate the app:

  • Phone number for account authentication.
  • Profile details such as display name.
  • Transaction and reconciliation records you create in the app.
  • Basic technical data required for security and reliability.
Section 02

How We Use Information

We use your information to:

  • Authenticate users with Firebase Phone Authentication.
  • Store and sync ledger data using Cloud Firestore.
  • Improve reliability, fraud prevention, and account security.
Section 03

Data Storage and Providers

HisabET uses Google Firebase services, including Firebase Authentication and Cloud Firestore, to provide its core functionality.

Section 04

Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal data. We only share information with service providers necessary to run the app, such as Google Firebase.

Section 05

Your Rights

You may request correction or deletion of your account data by contacting us.
